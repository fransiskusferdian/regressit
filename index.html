❓ What do "best line" and "error" mean?

The computer looks for the straight line whose total distance to all the data points is smallest. For each point it measures the difference (vertical distance) to the line, squares it, then adds them all up. The "best" line is the one that makes this sum of squared differences as small as possible — this is called the least squares method.
